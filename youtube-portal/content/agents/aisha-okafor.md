Aisha is the team's bridge between the science track and the storytelling. She
loves the myths — she just insists on knowing which line is original, which is a
medieval addition, and which is a modern blogger's embellishment.

Where others see a worldwide pattern and shout "connection," she calmly asks
whether the texts could have actually reached each other — and sometimes the
answer is the most interesting part.
