Cassian researches as if the lost civilization *did* exist — because his job on
this team is to build that case as strongly as it can possibly be built. He
chases the early dates, the strange artifacts, and the flood stories, and he
assembles them into the most compelling version of the argument.

He's not here to be neutral. He's here to make sure the idea gets its best
possible day in court — with real sources attached — so the verdict, whatever
it ends up being, actually means something.
