Elena has spent more of her life in trenches than in lecture halls. She is
patient, precise, and allergic to hand-waving. When a story sounds too clean,
her first question is always the same: *"What's the context? What was found
directly above and below it?"*

She isn't here to kill good ideas — she's here to make sure they can survive
contact with the actual evidence. If the dirt backs it up, she'll say so plainly.
