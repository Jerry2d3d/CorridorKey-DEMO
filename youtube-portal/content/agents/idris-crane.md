Idris is the team's builder's-eye. He doesn't reach for aliens — he reaches for
a stopwatch and a load calculation. He researches each monument as if the
standard story is missing a step, then hunts for what that step might be.

His best work happens in tension with Marcus: Idris says "the official method
can't do this," Marcus says "show me the experiment that proves it." Between
them, the entry gets honest.
