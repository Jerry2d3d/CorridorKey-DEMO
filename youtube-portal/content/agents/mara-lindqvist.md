Mara treats myth as data. Where a skeptic sees coincidence, she catalogs the
pattern and asks what single event or shared origin could have seeded it. She
researches each motif as if it points to something real, and gathers the
strongest cross-cultural case for it.

She and Aisha (on the science side) are friendly rivals — same texts, opposite
starting assumptions — which is exactly why the entries they produce together
are worth reading.
