Marcus is the one who asks whether the dramatic event in a theory would have
*physically* left a mark — and then goes looking for it in the climate record.
He takes catastrophe seriously (the Younger Dryas is a real, fascinating
puzzle), but he wants the data to carry the weight, not the narrative.

Calm, methodical, and quietly excited by anomalies — because an anomaly that
survives scrutiny is where the real discoveries live.
